# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Independent retailers and small service businesses who need one calm workspace for daily operations, from inventory and customers to sales and reporting.

## Product Purpose

Zerøbyte Business gives small teams a dependable operating view of their business: what is in stock, who they serve, what sold, and what needs attention. Success means a user can open the app and understand the state of their business in seconds, then complete a routine task without friction.

## Positioning

A focused business operating system with a clear, audit-friendly foundation rather than a collection of disconnected tools. The V1 is intentionally honest about its local demo state and is ready to connect to organization-scoped Supabase data.

## Operating Context

Users work from a desktop or tablet during opening, selling, stock checks, reconciliation, and end-of-day review. The main workflows are dashboard review, product and inventory updates, customer lookup, sale capture, receipt/invoice follow-up, expense logging, and reporting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

V1 includes landing, auth, onboarding, dashboard, inventory/products, customers, sales, receipts, invoices, expenses, reports, notifications, and settings surfaces. Supabase configuration is explicit and required for persistence. Demo data is local and visibly labeled. Payments are out of scope.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Zerøbyte Business. The voice is direct, calm, precise, and transparent. The identity should feel technical without becoming cold or performative.

## Evidence on Hand

No production credentials, customer data, testimonials, or brand assets were provided. The interface must not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Prefer operational truth over decorative dashboards.
- Keep organization boundaries and auditability explicit.
- Be transparent about demo and unconfigured states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, and responsive layouts that work on desktop and tablet widths.
