# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Independent retailers and small service businesses who need one calm workspace for daily operations, from inventory and customers to sales and reporting.

## Product Purpose

Zerøbyte Business gives small teams a dependable operating view of their business: what is in stock, who they serve, what sold, and what needs attention. Success means a user can open the app and understand the state of their business in seconds, then complete a routine task without friction.

## Positioning

A focused business operating system with a clear, audit-friendly foundation rather than a collection of disconnected tools. The V1 is intentionally honest about its local demo state and is ready to connect to organization-scoped Supabase data.

## Operating Context

Users work from a desktop or tablet during opening, selling, stock checks, reconciliation, and end-of-day review. The main workflows are dashboard review, product and inventory updates, customer lookup, sale capture, receipt/invoice follow-up, expense logging, and reporting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Today the product includes: a landing page and public pages (guide, support, status, promoters, about, legal) that share one header, footer and look; sign-in, onboarding, owner, team and promoter accounts; a dashboard that compares today with yesterday and with the same weekday last week; inventory with product import from a CSV spreadsheet, stock counts (every difference written to the stock history), reorder-point alerts shown inside the app, and Needs restocking / Out of stock filters; purchase orders and suppliers; customers with credit balances; sales (also offline); receipts on A4 and on 58 mm and 80 mm roll printers; invoices; expenses; Records grouped day by day with monthly and yearly totals (completed sales only, in the business's own time zone) and CSV export; reports with profit, best sellers and slow movers; branches and staff roles; notifications; a full "download all my data" ZIP of spreadsheets, free on every plan; and a public status page. Supabase configuration is explicit and required for persistence. Demo data is local and visibly labeled.

Deliberately not built yet (decisions, so nobody has to ask again):
- Collecting money from customers (payment links, a Who-owes-you reminder flow) and WhatsApp messages: later, once the company registration is complete and there are enough users. Alerts and reminders stay inside the app.
- Barcode scanning (a supermarket need) and product variants such as sizes: later. CSV import is the fast way to enter many products for now.
- A public pricing section: not until receiving payments from users is switched on.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Zerøbyte Business. The voice is direct, calm, precise, and transparent. The identity should feel technical without becoming cold or performative.

## Evidence on Hand

No production credentials, customer data, testimonials, or brand assets were provided. The interface must not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Prefer operational truth over decorative dashboards.
- Keep organization boundaries and auditability explicit.
- Be transparent about demo and unconfigured states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, and responsive layouts that work on desktop and tablet widths.
