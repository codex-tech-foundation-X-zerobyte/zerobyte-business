Privacy that is clear by design.

Last updated September 8, 2026. This policy describes the information Zerøbyte Business processes to provide its workspace, authentication, organization controls, and audit-friendly business tools.

Information we process

We process account identifiers such as email address, authentication timestamps, optional profile name and phone, organization membership, and operational records you enter: products, stock, customers, sales, receipts, invoices, expenses, branches, employees, and attendance.

How we use information

We use it to sign users in, keep records scoped to the correct organization, provide requested workflows, enforce permissions, prevent misuse, support audit logs, and maintain service reliability. We do not use business records to create invented metrics or advertising profiles.

Supabase and service providers

Zerøbyte uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, row-level security, and selected server-side functions. The browser receives only the public Supabase key; service-role credentials remain server-side.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained while the organization account needs them, subject to operational and legal requirements. Organization owners can request corrections or deletion through the service operator. Authentication deletion may also remove related profiles and organization memberships.

Payments and tracking

Payment processing is not implemented in this V1. We do not request card or bank details, and the product does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.
