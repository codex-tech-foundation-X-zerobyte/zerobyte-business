Small files, clearly explained.

Last updated September 8, 2026. Zerøbyte uses minimal browser storage needed to keep authentication and workspace preferences reliable.

Essential authentication storage

Supabase Auth stores a session in the browser so a signed-in user can remain signed in across refreshes. Clearing it signs the user out.

Workspace preference storage

Zerøbyte stores the selected organization and sidebar preferences in local storage. These values are preferences, not business records or credentials.

Service operation

The application may use browser cache and service-worker assets to support installability and faster loading. No advertising, cross-site tracking, or marketing cookies are used in this V1.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and site storage from browser settings. Doing so may sign you out and reset local preferences; it does not delete records stored in Supabase.

This notice describes the current V1 implementation and should be reviewed alongside the final production privacy policy.
